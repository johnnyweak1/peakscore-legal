Features

Everything your training needs

01Training Readiness

Wake up to a clear plan for today.

Overnight Peak Score reads your sleep, heart rate and recent training. In the morning it tells you to rest, keep it easy or push hard.

  • Stop guessing whether you're recovered
  • Your AI Coach explains what's behind each call
  • Plan ahead with tomorrow's forecast
  • Rest
  • Keep Easy
  • Push Hard

02Recovery Time

Go hard again at the right time.

After each session your Apple Watch counts down the hours your body needs to recover.

  • Long: give your body more rest
  • Fair: pick a lighter session
  • Short: go for high intensity
  • Long
  • Fair

03Workouts

Find out if your training is paying off.

Every workout your Apple Watch records, from lifting to running, with heart rate, effort and load in one place.

  • Rate each session: easy, hard or too much
  • Track the time you spend in each heart-rate zone
  • Catch fatigue before it turns into overtraining

04Workout Creator

Plan your next workout. Start it from your wrist.

Build a workout on your iPhone and send it to your Apple Watch.

  • Intervals with warm-up and cooldown
  • Goals for time, distance or calories
  • Schedule it for the day you train

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about your Apple Watch, your data and the AI Coach.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

Yes. Sleep, HRV, overnight HR and respiratory rate are measured by your Apple Watch.

Does my data leave my phone?

No. Analysis runs on-device. Your health data is never sold or shared.

What do I need for the AI Coach?

An iPhone with Apple Intelligence available and enabled.

Is Peak Score a medical device?

No. It provides fitness and educational insights, not medical advice.

Which athletes is it for?

Anyone who trains with an Apple Watch: gym, running, cycling, team sports or hybrid training.
